# 2026-07-26 — The Source meditation: before DN-003

The design meditation for Genesis-012 concluded. Nothing was implemented; nothing is authorized. This
entry fixes what the reflection established, because it reshaped the milestone before a line of it
exists.

## The demotion that makes the milestone

The reflection began with "the first inhabitant: a Source" and ended somewhere sharper: **the Source
is the narrative; the fact is the first difference of nature in world state.** Genesis-012's core is
heterogeneity — `Quantity` (affected by the phenomenon) and `Production` (affecting it) coexisting at
the same address, both persistent, both observable, both in principle transformable. The Source is
not a primitive, not a type, not a configured transition: it is a **recognised category** — a
deterministic predicate on declared state (`IsSource(a) := ProductionRate(a) > 0`). The name is
human; the recognition is objective. Genesis can have objectively true concepts without making them
fundamental.

## What is genuinely new (a precision)

Not "state causes change" — that was already true: redistribution's flux is determined by quantity
differences; the gradient was always a local reason the future would differ. What is new is double:
the Source is the first state **dedicated to causing** (its only role is causal), and the first
**causally asymmetric** state — it affects without being affected, neither consumed nor transported
by what it drives. A standing cause, not a circulating one. DN-003 should carry this precision so the
slogan never promises more than the proof.

## Conservation becomes accounting

`ΔTotal = ΣProduction`. The first theorem is not refuted; its domain of validity is revealed — it was
true of closed redistribution, and Genesis-012 opens the system. The constitutional reading: **the
kernel does not forbid creation; it forbids the inexplicable.** Every new unit of quantity is
causally chargeable to declared state.

## The law is universal; the state localises it

Decided in discussion: a Source must not live in the law-set (`SourceTransition(A, rate)` would make
the world lie about itself). The production law is generic — *declared production produces* — and can
be instantiated at **every** address, identical everywhere, inert wherever `rate = 0`. This resolves
the locality tension (no transition needs to enumerate the world's sources) and sharpens the
ablation: two worlds under **strictly identical laws**, differing only in state.

## The double ablation

- Remove `ProductionRate` (set 0): growth vanishes; redistribution continues. → the cause is in the
  world, not in the law.
- Remove the relations: production continues locally; transport vanishes. → origin, transport, and
  mechanism are three separable roles: Source → origin · Relations → transport · Redistribution →
  mechanism.

## The corpus was waiting for this milestone in three places

- **DN-001** spoke of *per-kind* resolvers; **008** recorded: "per-kind resolution becomes meaningful
  only when heterogeneous kinds exist." That moment is Genesis-012.
- **008** split identity from kind and made kind implicit (one kind: counter). 012 brings the kind
  axis back explicitly. The representation decision after DN-003 — state indexed by (identity) with
  structured values, or by (identity × kind) — is RFC-grade, and deliberately does **not** force the
  numeric-representation RFC (a rate is an integer; the questions stay separable).
- **The Glossary's Reserved *Entity* entry** stays Reserved — a Source is a local causal property,
  not an entity (no identity, no mobility, no persistence across space). A **new `Source` entry**
  becomes the first Active world category instead. Scope-honesty over ceremony.

## DN-003 — What is a Recognisable Pattern?

Title chosen (over "What is an Inhabitant?", which presupposes too much). Criteria sketched:
**state-grounded · deterministic · causally relevant · vocabulary-late · non-reifying · ablatable ·
scope-honest.** (A subtraction pass inside DN-003 may reduce these; seven is a draft, not a creed.)

## What Genesis-012 must not decide

Identity; entities fundamental-or-emergent; tracking mobile patterns; agents; external will; address
lifecycle; temporal patterns; category-as-compression; trees, animals, players; causal continuity.
The Source requires none of these — which is precisely why it is the right first concept.

## The proof object (compact forms)

> *Declared heterogeneous state can sustain the first recognisable world cause.*
>
> *The world can contain a cause of its own change.*

The sequence now reads: Genesis-011 — *the laws sustain a world*. Genesis-012 — *the world contains a
cause of its own change*. A stone would have occupied an address. The Source gives time a direction.

Next instrument: **DN-003**. The meditation stops here, deliberately, before it.
