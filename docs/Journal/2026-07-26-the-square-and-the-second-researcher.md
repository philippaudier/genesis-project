# 2026-07-26 — The square, mortality, and the second researcher

The closing entry of the founding cycle. Written after ADR-0004 was read back, when its author saw
what had actually been built.

## The square

The four ADRs are not a list; they are a structure. ADR-0001: *what is the world?* (architecture).
ADR-0002: *how is it honestly built?* (construction). ADR-0003: *how do we learn about it?*
(method). ADR-0004: *what is knowledge of it?* (epistemology). Every structural question now has an
answer.

One precision, verified against the corpus: the missing *Why* is not unanswered — it is held at a
**higher authority level**, deliberately. The Constitution's Preamble and its final rule (*does this
make the world itself better?*) are the why; they outrank every ADR and change almost never. The
square sits under the Constitution: structural answers below, purpose above. Whys change with
researchers; that is exactly why this one was placed where researchers cannot casually rewrite it.

## Documentary causality

The line *"mortal only by its own kill criterion, never by a theory's death"* does more than protect
RFCs. It defines a causal chain for documents: a theory can no longer kill a decision directly — it
must pass through prediction, observation, and reduction. **Authority has been taken from ideas and
returned to observations. The world must always intervene.**

## Mortality

The unnoticed common property of the six scientific objects: each has a death condition.
Observation — refutation. Hypothesis — counter-observation. Theory — repeated predictive failure.
Debt — paid or forgiven. RFC — its kill criterion. Immature disciplines explain how to create;
mature ones also explain how to let die. Genesis now documents both directions of every idea's life
— which makes the discipline falsifiable *as a whole*, not just piece by piece.

## Self-correction

Debts that demand payment, kill criteria that specify destruction, observations that outrank
explanations: together they produce an emergent property — the corpus is becoming
**self-correcting**. Not because its people are careful, but because its structure makes certain
corrections mandatory. The method no longer depends only on persons.

## Prediction P-001 — the second researcher

Logged formally, as ADR-0004 now permits: risky, specific, testable — untestable on demand, which is
what makes it honest.

> **One day, someone who knew neither of the founders nor Observation-004 will open the laboratory,
> follow the corpus, and produce documents with the same style of reasoning — not because it was
> anyone's style, but because of the corpus's internal gravity.**

Its test, when it comes: the hard-to-fake signatures. Not vocabulary mimicry — anyone can write
"kill criterion." The distinguishing evidence will be *behavioural*: a hypothesis refuted without
drama; a reduction honestly failed; a deferral catalogued instead of decided; happiness at a
refutation. If those appear in a stranger's document, the prediction is confirmed — and Genesis will
have achieved something harder than building a world: **transmitting a way of discovering.**

If instead the corpus produces only imitation of its surface forms, the prediction dies, and its
death will be recorded like every other.

The founding cycle closes here. The square stands under the Constitution; every object can die; the
debts are on the ledger; the world turns. What comes next is not a document. It is whoever opens the
laboratory next.
