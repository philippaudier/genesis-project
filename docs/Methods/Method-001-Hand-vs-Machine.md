# Method-001 — Hand vs Machine

> The Methods register holds **reusable experimental protocols**, each earned by a validating
> campaign — never written in advance of one. It is founded with exactly this file (the DN-005
> discipline: an empty registry is a vocabulary-late violation). The boundary with the ADRs is
> deliberate: ADR-0002/0003 hold the method's *law* (how Genesis builds and learns — the
> Reduction Test lives there); this register holds *operational protocols* a campaign can adopt
> by citation.

Validated by: **Campaign S1-002 — The First Flow** (2026-07-29; six of six sealed hand
derivations exact to the tick across twelve worlds; Observation-013).

## What it tests

**An understanding — not a world.** A deterministic system can still be opaque. This method
measures whether the laboratory can *calculate* its object before looking at it. It answers:
"do we merely run the kernel, or do we comprehend it?"

## When it applies

Campaigns whose worlds are **fully derivable by hand** from the law text — minimal worlds,
bounded runs. Where derivation stops being feasible, the method stops applying — and that
boundary is itself a datum: **the frontier of hand-computability maps the frontier of the
laboratory's direct understanding.**

## The protocol

1. **Manual Derivation Boundary** — every reference calculation must be reproducible from the
   law/fixture text alone: no execution, no consultation of any prior result.
2. **Claims = derivations, sealed before execution.** Late slots are permitted but must be
   filled, by hand, at the seal (the blind-slot tradition).
3. **E4 — The Hand Cannot Blink.** Once execution begins, the derivation is intangible. The
   world is compared to the derivation that existed before it spoke — never to a reconstructed
   one. Errors discovered mid-run are recorded as World Corrections, not repaired.
4. **A consistency witness runs first.** An independent instrument (S1-002: the per-edge flux
   counter's per-cell balance check) must certify the record before any timing is trusted —
   and the instrument must previously have proven it can *fail* (a B3-style negative control:
   an instrument that cannot scream cannot testify).
5. **Three layers, kept apart, all sealed:** quick intuitions (informal expectations, non-
   scored) · hand derivations (the claims) · the world (the record). Each pair-wise
   disagreement is information; the world arbitrates.

## Attribution of a mismatch (bound in the protocol)

Re-derive by hand first. An arithmetic error is a World Correction. A *correct* derivation
that still mismatches the record means the mechanism-hypothesis is incomplete — evidence, not
embarrassment.

## Validation record

S1-002: C0–C5 all confirmed, exact (threshold sweep 2/1/0/0/0; stars k→k; cascade 2,5,9,14;
locality pair 14/13); Consistency Check 0 mismatches × 12 worlds; one quick intuition
(constant cascade delay) lost to the hand derivation, and the world sided with the hand —
the three-layer separation observed working in a single campaign.

## Limits

- Does not scale past hand-computability (by construction).
- Validates *predictive* understanding only; it says nothing about a mechanism's uniqueness
  (another mechanism deriving the same ticks would pass identically — the Reduction Test, not
  this method, adjudicates between mechanisms).
- One validating campaign so far; a second science's use will test whether the method travels.

## Standing caution (founder's, 2026-07-29)

This method exerts a gravitational pull: a laboratory that knows how to calculate may
unconsciously begin choosing **only calculable campaigns**. Sciences do not exist to confirm
their capacity to calculate; they exist to meet the places where they stop understanding. A
campaign may be *chosen because it threatens this method* — never merely because it suits it.
