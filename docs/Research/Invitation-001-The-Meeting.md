## M1 — New hypotheses (had they been imagined?)

**Convergent core** (independently re-derived, never shown): the half-Laplacian map I − L/2; the
λ = 4 threshold and the three regimes; the asymptotic ratio law (their λ_max/2 − 1 is the first
researcher's |1 − μ_max/2|, identical); freeze ⇔ all gradients ≤ 1; the floors as the second-order
mechanism; the scale-reading of the ×4096 experiment. The core of CT-003 was rebuilt from the
facts alone, in one pass.

**Never imagined by the first researcher** — six new hypotheses, four of which land directly on
open debts:

1. **The overshoot pump**: negativity requires a vertex simultaneously above ≥ 3 neighbours;
   degree-≤2 worlds starting nonnegative can *never* go negative. (Touches RD-4 — and the
   legality jurisprudence: a structural class of worlds where Q ≥ 0 is a theorem, not a
   constraint.)
2. **Arithmetic glass** (RD-2): the ≈2⁶² freeze is a wrapped comparison failing the `> 0` guard —
   frozen by overflow artefact, not by plateau. Mechanistic, checkable by inspection.
3. **Period-2-only as a theorem of real spectra** (RD-3): symmetric operator → real eigenvalues →
   no rotation → sign flip is the only non-decaying oscillation. An *argument*, where the corpus
   had only a tally.
4. **Sourced period-3 as a floor-residue cycle** (RD-5): shape-periodicity as residue dynamics on
   a finite state space, categorically distinct from linear modes.
5. **Automorphism silence** (Obs-002/RD-6-adjacent): symmetric-by-construction places stay equal
   forever; their edges cannot fire — dead edges as a symmetry theorem.
6. **The centre-minimum law** −(V−3)/2 and the **O(amplitude) vs O(log amplitude)** damping split
   — quantitative laws the first researcher measured but never derived.

## M3 — New blind spots (implicit assumptions nobody noticed?)

Four genuine hits, conceded:

- **Parity was confounded with divisibility**: every persistent even load ever run (12, 120, 1200)
  was a multiple of 12. The corpus's "parity split" rests on a confound the record cannot exclude.
- **Single-point loads only**: superposition — the most natural probe of a near-linear system —
  was never tested; nor mixed-sign initial states.
- **The survey buckets don't close**: 21+55+413 = 489 of 1200; the dossier (and the original
  observation) never accounted for the 711 silent worlds, and the discriminating variable (each
  world's spectrum) was never recorded against outcome.
- **Directed relations**: permitted by the law since Genesis-009, used by no experiment ever.

## M4 — Convergence

**The mathematics converged; the ontology diverged.** Both theories say: linear skeleton, spectral
fate, floors as the second mechanism. But they disagree about which object is fundamental:

- CT-003 (first researcher): *activation governs transitions between episodes; the spectrum
  governs each locked episode.* The firing pattern is the primary object.
- The deposit (second researcher): *the firing-set lock is a shadow — the dominant eigenvector's
  sign pattern flipping in unison.* The spectrum is primary; activation is derived, and the
  two-timescale look is mode-crossover arithmetic.

This is exactly the inversion question raised after Observation-010 — now held by two researchers
on opposite sides, each with a death condition on record (the deposit's "firing-set lock" clause
vs CT-003's A1). It is a real, testable disagreement, and it was produced by independence, not by
assignment.
