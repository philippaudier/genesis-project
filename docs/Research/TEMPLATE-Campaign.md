# Campaign-XXX — Title

> Canonical campaign protocol (adopted 2026-07-28, S1-001 review). A campaign is designed in
> full, sealed by commit, then executed under separate authorisation. Sections in order; a
> section deliberately left empty says so — an empty slot is data.

Status: Designed / **Sealed at `commit`** / Executed / Closed (outcome)

```text
Question
  ↓
Blind Spot Audit            (what is the corpus NOT equipped to see? — before anything is sealed)
  ↓
Predictions (claims)        (per-claim table; outcomes bound in advance, per claim)
  ↓
Second imagination          (when a theory is on trial: the rival's column, equal rights — L5)
  ↓
Common Ground               (what all imaginations predict identically — agreement is knowledge too)
  ↓
Expectations (informal)     (sealed, non-scored — the only list Failed Expectations may read)
  ↓
Confidence declaration      (per claim, whose they are — feeds the calibration record)
  ↓
SEAL (commit)
  ↓
Implementation              (fixtures + instruments, versioned in the repo before execution —
                             the harness is part of the proof; laws are instruments, not corpus)
  ↓
Execution                   (separate authorisation, always)
  ↓
Observations                (facts only, house form)
  ↓
Unexpected Manifestations   (world phenomena nobody predicted; "None" is an observation;
                             no baptism — vocabulary-late)
  ↓
Failed Expectations         (the non-happened — scored ONLY against sealed expectations/claims;
                             an absence is a datum only against a fully observed world)
  ↓
World Corrections           (what the world/corpus forced the laboratory to correct along the way —
                             the story of the research, not of the world)
  ↓
Reduction                   (findings tried against existing primitives before any design)
  ↓
Decision
```

Standing rules:

- **A campaign is never a failure for refuting its hypothesis.** It fails only if it cannot
  produce an exploitable answer. *(Jurisprudence: Run-000003, Campaign-001 Outcome D, RFC-L003,
  LB-001. Pending as an ADR-0003 amendment upon a fifth instance — ideally from a science.)*
- **Seal-then-run**: git ancestry proves record antecedence — of the predictions AND of the
  experimental world.
- Post-seal, pre-execution personal predictions may be filed and dated (the Campaign-002
  precedent); they are scored apart.
