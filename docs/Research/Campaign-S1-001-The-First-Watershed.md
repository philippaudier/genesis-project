## Question

> **Can a watershed appear as an emergent phenomenon — do candidate laws suffice to make it
> manifest, without any law knowing the landscape?**

## What this campaign does NOT try to prove

- It does not seek a beautiful terrain.
- It does not simulate a realistic river.
- It does not validate geomorphology.
- It does not validate Science-001.
- **No law used here enters the corpus by being used.** Gravity, flow, erosion are *fixtures* —
  laboratory instruments, world content of the experimental parcels. A law earns corpus status
  only by surviving a Reduction Test, never by appearing in a campaign.

## Blind Spot Audit (before seal)

What the corpus is NOT equipped to see, declared now:

1. **The transfer assumption.** The studied law family moved the *carried quantity itself*
   downhill. Here the driving potential is composite: `potential := Elevation + Water`, while
   only Water moves. The corpus's regime map is claimed to transfer under this identification —
   that claim is itself under trial (see A0/W0: a flat-relief control collapses the composite
   case onto the exact corpus family, separating transfer-failure from theory-failure).
2. **Scale.** Corpus worlds had ≤ ~13 vertices. These parcels have 27–135 places. Spectral
   *density* (not just radius) is untested territory.
3. **Distributed sources.** Corpus sources were single-point (a declared confound); rain is a
   crossing into many cells at once. The parity/divisibility confound of Genesis-019 is NOT
   resolved by this campaign — deliberately left for a targeted one.
4. **Read-mostly coupling.** Elevation is read by the flow fixture but written only by the
   erosion fixture (W4). A kind that parameterises another kind's dynamics has never existed.
5. **The reader.** Watershed detection is a new instrument and can overclaim (RD-L6's lesson
   transfers). Its output is bound below to declared conventions; forbidden vocabulary is
   test-enforced.

## The worlds (fixtures — all parameters bound at seal)

All parcels: 4-neighbour grids (von Neumann); kinds `E` (elevation), `W` (water), plus `R`/`S`
(rock/sediment) and the sacrificial kind in W4. Kind identifiers are opaque to the kernel.
Additive resolvers on all kinds except B3's sacrificial one. Rain = declared membrane crossings,
uniform `+1` to `W` at every cell, ticks 0–19 inclusive, then closed. Potential := E + W.

- **W0 — Flat Control.** 9×9, E ≡ 0. The composite potential degenerates to the corpus family
  exactly. Claims: A0.
- **W1 — Naive Bowl.** 9×9, E = bowl (centre 0, +1 per ring outward, rim 4). Naive flow fixture
  (corpus family, no degree correction). Claims: A1, A5. Run long enough to observe divergence
  onset; abort well before the 2⁶² frontier (Obs-008 on the map).
- **W2 — Corrected Bowl.** Same parcel, degree-aware divisor fixture. Claims: A2, A3, A4.
- **W3 — Twin Basins.** 15×9; two bowls separated by a ridge of E = 5 along the middle column;
  corrected fixture. **The watershed witness.** The reader computes the *terminal-minimum
  partition*: for each place, the basin floor its water-flow path terminates at, from the record
  alone. The word "watershed" never appears in output — the partition is the fact; the name
  stays in the laboratory. Claims: A3, A4; informal expectations 1–4.
- **W4 — First Conversion.** 9×3 inclined channel (E descending along the long axis). Erosion
  fixture: where per-tick outflow of `W` across an edge exceeds threshold 4, emit the pair
  (−1 `R`, +1 `S`) at the source place — one law, one atomic emission (H1). Transport/deposit of
  `S` by the corrected flow driven by the same potential. Claims: B1, B2, B3 (B3 in a variant
  run with the sacrificial kind). Informal expectation 6.

## Fixture Transparency (deposited as an implementation discipline, not an RFC)

executing GravityFixture / FlowFixture / ErosionFixture — it executes a **FixtureSet**. S1-002
will replace laws; that operation must be an instrument substitution, never a rewrite of the
laboratory.
