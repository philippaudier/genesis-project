# Research Debts

*A discovery creates a debt of understanding. This ledger records them. A debt is not technical debt
— it is its inverse: the questions an observation opened without closing. Debts are **paid by
observations**, never by design. None of them, as recorded, requires an RFC.*

*Statuses: **Open** · **Paid** (by Observation-NNN) · **Forgiven** (the question dissolved).*
