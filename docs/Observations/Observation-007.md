## Observation

- **The depth-2 tree DIVERGES** — amplitudes grow without bound (|values| > 10⁹ by t=48) — although
  **no node exceeds degree 3**, the degree that Observation-005 measured as marginal (λ = −1) in
  single stars. Divergence therefore occurs strictly below the degree threshold that single-star
  worlds exhibited.
- The 6-ring (all degree 2) damps to **perfect equality** (2,2,2,2,2,2) by t=5, no negativity — as
  the 3-ring did (Observation-001).
- The 8-chain freezes into a **staircase gradient** (6,5,4,3,3,2,1,0) by t=15, no negativity — the
  far end still at exactly 0.
- The wheel (hub degree 4) diverges, consistent with Observation-005.

## Measurements

Star-of-stars growth ratio (centre extremes): ≈ ×1.45 per alternation — between the single-star
deg-3 (×1.0) and deg-4 (×1.5) rates.

## Hypotheses

*(kept apart)* **Observation-005's "degree is the master factor" is too local.** A world of
degree-≤3 nodes can diverge when its stars are *coupled*: stability appears to be a property of the
whole topology's spectrum (plausibly the graph's Laplacian eigenvalues under the flux rule), for
which single-star node degree was only a proxy. Consequence if confirmed: no *local* inspection of a
world (per-node degree checks) can predict whether it is stable — instability, like the phenomena
before it, is global and emergent. Obs-005's regime map stands for single stars; its explanatory
hypothesis needs the spectral generalisation. Untested beyond these topologies.
