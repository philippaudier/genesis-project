# Why Genesis Exists

> *« Ce texte existe déjà. Éparpillé dans toutes nos conversations. »*
> *« Il ne sera pas rédigé. Il sera monté. »*
> *« Chaque phrase doit avoir un ancêtre. »*

No sentence below is new. Each is quoted from the corpus — a document, a commit, an
observation, a conversation — and each carries its ancestor. The work was not writing.
The work was the order.
