# L-008 — Interference

*Not "Interaction". Interference. L-006: two biographies can coexist. L-007: a biography can
return. L-008 asks what happens when their continuities truly cross.*

Status: **Prediction sealed; implementation not yet begun.** (Committed before any code — the
ancestry is the proof.)

## The scientific question

> **When does a biography become a cause in another biography?**

Not "when do two bodies meet". Not "when do they exchange". But: at what moment does another body
begin to modify *my* story?

## What becomes observable

> **The failure of an expectation.** Until now, the Traveller always finds what it left. L-008
> introduces the possibility that the world answers: *no.*

## The illusion the laboratory could lose

> **The illusion that a memory of the world suffices.** Today the Traveller remembers. Tomorrow
> that may no longer be enough — because the world is now shared.

## What is built (the absolute minimum)

1. The player can take the sword left at the station. Nothing else. No chest, no extra mechanic.
2. **No modification of the Traveller's brain. None.** It returns; it walks exactly as before.
3. Two cases now exist at its return: sword present → it takes it back; sword absent → `…`
   **The "…" is deliberately left empty.** No alternative search, no frustration, no failure
   behaviour, no new goal — we do not even know yet whether a phenomenon appears.

**The founder's preference, adopted as a constraint: no new law if a restriction can be lifted
instead.** (Minimum Phenomenon Principle — this milestone is its candidate second demonstration.)

## What must NOT be built

Emotions · reputation · trust · relationship · internal memory · adaptive search. All of these
must earn their existence.

## The founder's sealed prediction (filed before one line of code)

> **The first major phenomenon will probably not be the absence of an object. It will be the
> appearance of a responsibility.** The player will say something like *"ah… c'est de ma faute"*
> — not because an NPC is sad, not because a dialogue says so, but because they recognise that
> another body's trajectory now depends on a gesture they made earlier. *(The founder: "I may of
> course be wrong. I almost hope I am.")*

*Scoring rules, declared now:* scored on the first human L-008 run — does behaviour or narrative
register responsibility (causal ownership of the other's outcome)? Never scored on scripted
co-runs. Between the run and its narrative, the laboratory says nothing. **And the Era II
extension, adopted at this milestone: a sealed prediction can now also be refuted by a completely
unexpected phenomenon — the question is no longer only "were we right?" but "what did we forget
to imagine?"**

## The protocol (now standard)

Sealed prediction (independent commit) → minimal implementation → headless verification → Unity →
first human run blind → no comment, no statistic, no interpretation between run and narrative →
narrative verbatim → only then: scoring, surprises, possible debt.
