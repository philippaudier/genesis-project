# Genesis-015 — The Constraint Expedition

*The first milestone that adds no capability to the world. Only knowledge.*

## Purpose

> **Determine whether the world contains phenomena capable of distinguishing competing legality
> policies.**

Not: implement Constraints. Not: answer Q1. Only: **earn the right to ask Q1.**

## Motivation

RFC-0004 established that Constraints are irreducible. DN-004 established how they are declared.
ADR-0003 established that evidence precedes design. What remains unknown is not the implementation —
it is the phenomenon. The corpus contains one family of specimens (Observations 004–005); this is
insufficient to distinguish competing legality policies. Genesis therefore suspends implementation
and returns to the laboratory.

## Success criterion

Not "Constraint system implemented," but:

> **Produce at least one observation whose interpretation differs under two candidate legality
> policies.**

The milestone succeeds even if Q1 remains unanswered. It fails only if no distinguishing specimen is
found.

## Non-goals

No Constraint implementation; no legality code; no kernel change; no answer to Q1–Q6. Everything
remains frozen.

## Expeditions

- **A — High-degree worlds.** Degree changes stability; what else does it change?
- **B — Random worlds.** Thousands of deterministic worlds, searched automatically. No hypothesis;
  collect anomalies.
- **C — Cycles inside stars.** Nested topology; does legality appear locally before globally?
- **D — Mixed sources.** Competing inflows; can legality become contextual?
- **E — The overflow frontier.** Observation-005 warned that divergence eventually destroys Int64.
  What does legality mean near numeric collapse? Observation, not implementation.

## Recording rule

Every expedition produces only measurements, observations, hypotheses. Never RFC text, implementation
ideas, or policy discussion — those are downstream. (Policy *interpretation* of specimens belongs to
the milestone's exit analysis, not to the observation files.)

## Exit criteria

- **Outcome A** — a specimen separates two legality policies → Q1 becomes *Ready*.
- **Outcome B** — every observed phenomenon is policy-equivalent → Q1 remains *Premature*. Still
  success: negative evidence is evidence.
- **Outcome C** — a phenomenon destroys RFC-0004 → reopen the RFC. Scientific victory.

## The trajectory

Genesis-013 built the instrument. Genesis-014 built the language. Genesis-015 builds **the
patience** — the refusal to answer before the world permits it. The milestone does not promise an
artifact. It promises intellectual honesty: to reduce uncertainty without pretending it has
disappeared.

---

## Exit record (2026-07-26 — same day; the laboratory was faster than the plan again)

All five expeditions ran headless (zoo of 8 topologies; 2,000 seeded random worlds; the overflow
frontier crossed deliberately). Results in Observations 006–008.

**Outcome A is achieved — Q1 becomes Ready.** The distinguishing specimens exist, in abundance:
violations fall into three temporal classes (transient self-healing — 21/1200 closed worlds;
persistent-periodic — 55/1200; divergent — 413/1200), and candidate legality policies produce
*different futures* per class: a halting policy denies transient-class worlds the legal equilibrium
they reach unaided; a tolerant policy leaves the persistent class illegal forever. No policy is
uniformly innocent — which is precisely what makes Q1 a real question now.

Two bonuses, one of each polarity:
- **A refinement** (Obs-007): a max-degree-3 world diverges — stability is spectral, not local;
  Obs-005's regime facts stand, its degree hypothesis was too local.
- **A refutation** (Obs-008): divergence does *not* destroy the accounting — conservation holds even
  through Int64 wraparound (the theorem lives in modular arithmetic); the collapse at the frontier
  is semantic, not arithmetic. Negative evidence, recorded as evidence.

Nothing was implemented. The kernel is untouched. Q1 is not answered — it is, at last, *earned*.
