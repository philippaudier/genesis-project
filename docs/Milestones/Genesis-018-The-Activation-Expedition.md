# Genesis-018 — Campaign-002: The Activation Expedition

*The method returns to the world. RD-8 becomes the centre of gravity.*

## Purpose

> Determine what decides that an episode becomes spectral — and map exactly where each claim of the
> merged working candidate holds, and where it stops.

Campaign-001 asked which theory described the observations best. Campaign-002 asks a more
fundamental question: it no longer tries to crown an explanation, it tries to **chart its domain of
validity**. The expected best outcome is not "CT-003 was right" but *"here is exactly where each
claim ceases to be valid."*

## The Genesis-017 contract, applied

This is the first campaign bound by the Laboratory Expedition's five deltas — its design *is* the
test of Genesis-017's exit criterion:

1. **Per-claim verdicts** (L3): four claims A1–A4, four independent verdicts, no winner row.
2. **Shared Blind Spot Audit** (L5): run at design time; found something sharper than expected —
   not "why do relations die" (that is A1/A4's own subject) but **orbit selection**: why period 2
   and never another, why this frozen state — RD-3 resurfacing as the merger's inherited blind
   spot. W2 is instrumented against it. The campaign's lack of an adversary theory is declared as
   a limitation, with its consequence (smooth confirmation ⇒ suspect the missing adversary).
3. **Conventions pre-registered** (L8): active edge, activation pattern, episode, freeze tick,
   ratio, and the in-domain bound |values| < 2⁴⁰ (RD-2's régime excluded, not explained).
4. **Confidence declarations continue** (L4): per-claim, with death conditions, sealed at design.
5. **The seal claims what a seal proves** (L1): record antecedence, not epistemic blindness.

## Design corrections made before sealing

- **A1 was corrected against published data**: activation converges to *periodic orbits*, not fixed
  patterns — the even star's alternation refutes the stronger wording without running anything.
- **W4 was redesigned**: "artificially frozen activation" would have required a control-law
  fixture; a scale argument (sign structure is load-invariant, truncation is not) yields the same
  discriminator with the existing law. CT-003 is forced to bet that DP-3's early excursions
  *persist* under ×4096 load — it can die on its own native ground.

## Deliverables

All delivered in **Campaign-002** (`docs/Research/Campaign-002-The-Activation-Expedition.md`):
CT-003 (working, unnamed) with four separated claims; four worlds (quiet chain, activation warfare,
gradient frontier, scale control); sealed per-claim predictions, confidences and death conditions;
bound per-claim verdict structure. One claim direction (A4 ⇐) is filed as a theorem candidate
derived from the law's text alone.

## Exit

Genesis-018 completes when Campaign-002's verdicts are recorded and RD-8's ledger entry is updated
— paid in part, or deepened. Execution is a separate act, authorised separately. Naming is not
triggered by any outcome; DN-005 stands untouched.
