# Genesis-019 — The Invitation

*The first expedition whose observed system is neither the world nor the laboratory, but the
laboratory's transmissibility.*

## Question

> **Can Genesis produce genuinely independent theories when given to another researcher under
> controlled information?**

Not "will another researcher agree?", not "who is better?". Only: **is the laboratory reproducible
at the level of imagination?**

## Why now

Observation-010 closed a loop: Campaign-002 confirmed every claim of the working candidate, and the
protocol itself concluded that the absent adversary is the first suspect. This is no longer an
idea; it is an empirical consequence. Genesis-019 is the laboratory answering its own clause.

## Hypothesis

None — deliberately. Genesis-019 does not confirm; it measures what appears when an independent
imagination enters the laboratory.

## Protocol

**The second researcher receives** (the Evidence Dossier, committed *before* the invitation so its
exact content is auditable): the law and kernel semantics as world-facts; the experimental
conventions; every observed configuration and its measured data — trajectories, freeze ticks,
ratios, firing-pattern logs, counts.

**The second researcher does not receive:** CT-001, CT-002, CT-003; any theory discussion; the
interpretation journals; the calibration records; the research debts as formulated explanations;
any interpretive vocabulary (no "spectral", "orbit", "episode", "regime names"). **Facts are
shared. Explanations are not.**

**The mission — one only:** construct the best possible explanation of the evidence; then deposit
predictions for *new, unrun* experiments, with confidences and death conditions — never having seen
the existing theories. No harness execution before the deposit: the deposit must be blind exactly
as the laboratory's own seals are.

**Discipline:** no debate before the deposit; no cross-reading; no adaptation. Two independent
stories. Then, and only then: the meeting.

## Declared limitations (they bound what this can prove)

1. **The curator is conflicted.** The dossier's author also authored CT-003; curation shapes
   imagination. Remedy: the dossier is sealed by commit before the invitation, and contains only
   uninterpreted facts.
2. **Same substrate.** The second researcher is a fresh context of the same model, not a different
   mind. What is isolated is *information*, not cognitive architecture. Genesis-019 therefore tests
   transmissibility across an information barrier — the weakest, and currently only available, form
   of the second-imagination requirement. A stronger form (a genuinely different mind) remains
   open.

## What Genesis measures

- **M1 — New hypotheses.** Had they already been imagined?
- **M2 — New predictions.** Experiments nobody had thought of?
- **M3 — New blind spots.** Does the researcher point at an implicit assumption nobody noticed?
- **M4 — Convergence.** When the theories finally meet: converge, diverge, or complement?

## Outcomes (fixed before the invitation)

- **A** — Same theory, same predictions: the laboratory is transmissible.
- **B** — Same theory, new predictions: the laboratory enriches its own theory.
- **C** — Different theory, same evidence: historic — two honest competing explanations, and the
  adversary the suspicion clause demanded now exists. Both theories get identical rights and the
  identical protocol.
- **D** — The researcher finds a missing observation: possibly the best result — the method reveals
  absences too.

**Not permitted:** "who was right?". That is not the subject.

## Exit

> **The Invitation neither validates nor refutes a theory. It validates — or refutes — the
> laboratory's ability to produce independent imagination from shared evidence.**

Genesis-019 completes when the deposit is recorded verbatim, the meeting is scored on M1–M4, and
the outcome (A–D) is bound by the pre-registered definitions above.
