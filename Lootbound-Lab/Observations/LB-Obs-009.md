## The suspension discovery (the account's most novel content)

```
"figé dans le temps quand j'étais hors du monde" — the world continues to EXIST without its
inhabitant, but it does not continue to LIVE: real time flows only during sessions. The era's
question — "que ressent-on en revenant quelque part qui a continué à vivre sans nous ?" —
quietly assumes an autonomy the world does not yet have. Persistence ≠ autonomy. The gap is
now visible, named, and NOT built: if the world one day needs to live unwatched, that demand
must arrive the way persistence did — from narratives.
```

## Unexpected phenomenon

```
A de facto producer policy emerged from the implementation, exercised by real data on both
paths: quit during the visit → the being resumes when you return; quit after its departure →
it is gone from that world forever. Nobody decided this; the guard plus the fresh-producer
restart produced it. It is lawful, observable — and it means WHEN you leave decides whether
you ever see the being again in that world. The window's closing time became world-fate.
```
