# The Watchlist — first observations to catch

*These are not observations. They are empty slots: moments the laboratory commits, in advance, to
noticing when they first occur. None speaks of DPS, loot, or statistics. All speak of behaviour.
When one happens, it becomes LB-Obs-NNN, recorded with the TEMPLATE, and its slot is marked
filled.*

| Slot | The moment to catch | Status |
|---|---|---|
| LB-Obs-001 | The first object the player refuses to replace | **Waiting** |
| LB-Obs-002 | The first place where the player stands still with no objective | **Waiting** |
| LB-Obs-003 | The first spontaneous screenshot | **Waiting** |
| LB-Obs-004 | The first story told after the session | **Waiting** |
| LB-Obs-005 | The first voluntary detour | **Waiting** |

*A slot may stay empty forever. An empty slot is data too.*
