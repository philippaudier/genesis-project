watching. The world's record cannot say what the stillness was; it can say when it was: exactly

```

A2  The window made absence impossible: a display that broadcasts every location grants an
    omniscience the world's bodies do not have — nothing can be missing for someone who always
    sees where everything is. The prediction failed against the INSTRUMENT, not the world.
A3  The contamination pre-satisfied the absence (already known, hence untellable as surprise),
    leaving presence as the only news.
A4  Novelty: anything moving that is not oneself would have won, this once.
```
