# Protocol-H001 — First Human Run

Status: **Sealed by this commit. Not yet run.** Not a campaign, not a study: the first contact.

## Objective

The laboratory does not seek to observe a player. It seeks to observe **what changes when a human
replaces a bot.**

## Prerequisite

The Unity EditMode suite is green (the world must be trusted before it hosts a person).

## Pre-registration

Deliberately: **none.** No prediction, no strategy, no objective is written before the session.
The researcher knows the hypotheses too well; silence is the best remaining protection.

## During the session

No additional instrumentation. No popup. No visible metric. No reminder. The world plays; the
laboratory records. (The observer's only lab affordance is the S key: export the session. Nothing
on screen refers to observation.)

## End of session

Exactly one question, then silence:

> **What stayed with you?**

The answer is pasted verbatim into the run file. No correction, no rewording, no follow-up.

**Declared limitation:** the subject is the laboratory's own founder, and the question is
self-administered. This is Run-000002's inherent conflict; it is declared, not solved. Future runs
with other producers will not carry it.

## The four objects produced

1. **The trace** — the world's record of every crossing.
2. **The biography** — the Chronicler's reading (whose vocabulary is itself under study, RD-L6).
3. **The observations** — LB-Obs entries derived later, in the house form.
4. **The narrative** — verbatim. Protected exactly as fiercely as the other three.

## What the laboratory then does

It does **not** look for a theory. It compares. Only.

```
Run-000001 (Bot)   ↔   Run-000002 (Human)
same world · same laws · same membrane · two biographies · two narratives
```

First questions — gaps only, no "why":

- Do the biographies diverge?
- Do the narratives diverge?
- Are the events told the ones the world flags as remarkable?
- Does the Chronicler tell the same story as the player?

**If the player tells nothing at all: magnificent — that is an observation, possibly a very
important one.** The laboratory must be as ready to receive silence as narrative.

## After Run-000002

Nothing. No feature, no RFC, no new world. Several days of looking at four objects — they likely
already contain things nobody is thinking of today.
