# LB-002 — Ordinary Days

Status: **Sealed by its commit, before any protocol visit.** Unlike LB-001, this campaign needs no
build: World-002 already exists — a clearing, two swords, a shelter, a story in progress. The seal
proves record antecedence; after it, the laboratory stops discussing the campaign.

## Warning (not a rule)

The greatest danger of this campaign is talking about it. The ordinary is extraordinarily
fragile — a single expectation is enough to make it stop being ordinary. Every sentence added
before the visits plants an expectation in the inhabitant. This document is the last sentence.

## Filiation

LB-001 demonstrated that the ordinary could inhabit an **object**.
LB-002 asks whether the ordinary can inhabit a **day**.

Same experiment, one scale up. The founding sentence — *ordinary moments are the most precious
ones* — moves from the object to time itself.

## Question

> **Can a continuing world make one want to return without producing any remarkable event?**

## Hypotheses (stated here once, then unspoken until close)

- **Null:** the visits cease. (The observable form — refined at review from the earlier
  "ordinary sessions become repetitive", which no record could bind.)
- **Alternative:** the return itself becomes the event.

Pre-registration beyond this document: **none** — per the H-001 precedent: *the researcher knows
the hypotheses too well; silence is the last protection.*

## Method (made of subtractions)

During visits:

- No phenomenon is sought.
- No situation is provoked.
- No "test" is run.
- The only objective is: **inhabit the world.**

After each visit — before reading anything, before any chronicle, any log, any export — the
inhabitant writes the answer to the laboratory's only question:

> **What stayed with you?**

Then the carnet closes. Nothing else is written; nothing is read.

Observations are **opportunistic only**: one is opened only if a phenomenon appears spontaneously —
never because it was awaited. (Article 8 of the Constitution binds here.)

## The fundamental clause

> **A visit can never be caused by the protocol.**

Visits are never scheduled, never owed, never prompted. A protocol that obliges returning
manufactures the very variable under study — the return. Not returning must remain possible,
and not returning is data. This is Article 8 applied to the campaign that earned it.

## Traces (written, not read)

The world already records everything for free since L-009; the discipline is to not open the book.
Among what the record holds without anyone attending to it:

- time spent at the Shelter;
- revisits;
- paths taken;
- spontaneous manipulation of objects;
- end-of-session places;
- visit durations;
- returns after interruption;
- unrequested ceremonies.

**All traces stay sealed until the campaign closes.** They are opened early only if a spontaneous
phenomenon demands its record. The rarity of observations is itself a trace — **never an
objective**: a laboratory rewarded for writing nothing destroys its own experiment.

## Outcomes (fixed before any visit)

- **A** — A sufficiently unexpected phenomenon emerges spontaneously and justifies a new targeted
  campaign.
- **B** — Several ordinary visits succeed one another without a single observation being needed,
  and the visits continue. Silence is the result.
- **C** — The visits cease. The world was not enough; the null hypothesis is confirmed —
  **recorded, not rescued.** No relaunch, no reminder, no "come see what is happening." If
  World-002 dies, the laboratory writes *it happened*, never *let us start again*. The chronicle
  closes the way chronicles close.

*No outcome validates a design decision. Outcomes produce understanding; design changes require
their own justification later, citing observations.* (LB-001's closing line, unchanged.)

## The laboratory's own conduct

- The inhabitant is the founder. The visual field is the founder's.
- The laboratory does not visit: since L-009, a headless session would write into World-002's
  history — an intervention, precisely what this campaign forbids.
- Silence between a visit and its carnet entry, absolute (the contamination rule, standing since
  Run-000003).
- No mechanics are proposed while the campaign runs. The laboratory's success condition inverts:
  returning with nothing to report is, for once, the good outcome.
- A breach of any of the above is **declared**, not hidden — the Run-000003 precedent.

## Motto

> **Live first. Explain later.**

The inhabitant-side sibling of *No hidden hand*. It names a practice that already existed —
narrative before export, silence before reading — which is how this house baptises: vocabulary
last, never first.
