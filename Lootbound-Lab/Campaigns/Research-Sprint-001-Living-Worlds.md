# Research Sprint 001 — Living Worlds

Status: **OPEN.** Code frozen at commit `2841e82` (L-008). The first sprint in the project's
history whose deliverable is *nothing built*.

> **Objective: build nothing while the world keeps producing phenomena.**

The laboratory is finally equipped — two persistent bodies, a shared world, real interference, two
sealed predictions waiting, a deterministic experimental control, 92 green tests, three reading
instruments (Trace, Biography, Narrative). It is time to do what it was built for. Observe.

## The freeze

No code. No new milestone. No new mechanic — not even a small one, not even an obviously good
one. If a lack becomes painful, that pain is *data*: write it down, do not fix it. The only
editable artefacts during the sprint: run files, observations, the carnet.

## Protocol, per run

1. Play. Blind — no strategy written, no prediction reread.
2. **Narrative first**: at session's end, write what stayed with you *before* opening any export
   or file. Then export (S), paste the narrative verbatim, rename the run file (Run-000005, 6, …).
3. Between any run and its narrative, the laboratory says nothing — now standard.
4. Each run is a specimen. Vary nothing deliberately; play as it comes. Interfering with the
   Traveller's cache, or not, is always your choice and never a task.

## The four sprint tracks

*(Numbered S1–S4 to avoid colliding with the standing Programs register P1–P3.)*

- **S1 — Recognition** *(feeds the L-007 sealed prediction, `cb6e6c8`)*
  When is another body recognised? Scoring per run: never / sometimes / systematically.
- **S2 — Responsibility** *(feeds the L-008 sealed prediction, `22f150b`)*
  When does a player attribute an event's cause to themselves? never / sometimes / systematically.
- **S3 — Interference**
  Which interferences appear spontaneously? Not only swords. Everything.
- **S4 — Silence** *(new — possibly the most interesting)*
  **What does the player deliberately ignore?** Absences already tell a lot; ignorings may tell
  more. (Scored only from behaviour and narrative; ignoring is claimed by the record, never by
  the reader — RD-L6 discipline applies.)

## Sprint disciplines

- **No debt is paid during the sprint.** They accumulate. Classification happens once, at the
  end. We finally know how to wait.
- **Observations gain a standing field** (added to the TEMPLATE, not the Constitution):
  `Unexpected phenomenon: None / …` — the protocol now officially recognises that the world can
  produce what nobody imagined, and captures it explicitly.
- Predictions are scored only at sprint end, on the accumulated record — except where a sealed
  scoring rule says "first human run", which binds as written.

## The end (after ~10 runs)

Gather — not the traces, not the biographies: **the surprises.** Then one question only:

> **What is the smallest law that would make possible the greatest number of observed phenomena?**

Not "which feature is missing?" — *which debt keeps returning?* The next milestone may not exist
yet. It is hidden in the next ten narratives. If the sprint ends and the world has demanded
nothing, that too is a result: the world is sufficient, and the laboratory keeps watching.

> *At the start, we invented the questions. Now the questions begin to emerge from the world
> itself. L-009 will not be chosen — it will be asked for.*

# SPRINT CLOSED — the synthesis (10 specimens: Runs 000005–000014)

## The surprises, gathered

1. Recognition debuted **inverted** (self mistaken for other) and ended **habituated** ("comme à
   son habitude…") — the full arc in ten runs.
2. The player **became the laboratory**: waiting posts, conditional protocols, manipulations.
3. An **arbitrary tiebreak was felt as punishment** — the world's least significant code produced
   its most charged sentence.
4. **Concealment appeared**: an interference undone so the other would not notice — belief
   management toward a machine that notices nothing.
5. The narrator **lives in a persistent world that does not exist** — six of ten narratives
   presuppose continuity across resets.
6. The reader's vocabulary **colonised the narrator** ("the other sword" inside French).
7. Desire inverted LB-001's expectation: the invested sword is "ma old sword"; the coveted one
   is the other's. And the other's sword was *repaired* by the player, twice, unprompted.
8. One attempted **aggression on the other's body** — the membrane has no such channel.

## The one question, answered by tally

> *What is the smallest law that would make possible the greatest number of observed phenomena —
> which debt keeps returning?*

- **Persistence across sessions** — demanded (as lived fiction) by 6 of 10 narratives: return,
  habit, "comme avant", concealment-from-future-notice. RFC-L003 was founded on day one and
  deferred until a world demanded it. **The demand-side document now exists, written by the
  founder's own runs.** Enables the most narrated phenomena by turning them from fiction to fact.
- **Choice in taking** — blocked desire in 3 runs (11, 12, 13): the player cannot choose which
  object a gesture touches, and cannot take at all where no gesture exists (the Field).
- **Acting on the other's body** — voiced once (run 9). Recorded, not yet insistent.

The world has spoken twice, loudly. **L-009 is the founder's choice between the two demands the
world actually voiced** — the laboratory presents the tally and stops.
