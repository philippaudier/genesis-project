# RD-11 probe — what happens after S1-004?

Status: **exploratory, contaminated, non-evidential**.

This probe extends the already observed M1 parcel of S1-004 beyond its sealed
six ticks. It exists only to find a resistance worth asking about:

- fixation;
- periodic shuttling;
- flattening;
- dispersion;
- or continued change not captured by those readings.

Because the parcel, laws, first trajectory, and S1-004 outcome were already
known when this probe was written, nothing produced here may be used as a
campaign specimen, an Observation, or evidence for RD-11. Any later campaign
must use an independently designed parcel and must declare this probe in its
blind-spot / contamination record.

The probe compares two state signatures:

1. `SolidSurface = Base + Rock + Sediment`, the observer's reading;
2. all four cell values (`Base`, `Rock`, `Sediment`, `Water`), excluding the
   tick number.

The distinction matters: a repeated surface does not imply that the world
itself has repeated.
