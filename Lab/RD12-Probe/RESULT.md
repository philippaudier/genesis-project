## The resistance exposed

The probe refutes its own useful simplification: competence is not, by itself,
the scalable explanation of rest.

Under the structurally stabilised divisor, competence 0 also rests. The
degree policy determines whether this contaminated extended family remains in
a usable dynamical domain; competence still changes **which distribution**
survives inside that domain.

The diagnostic changed the shared divisor for Water flow, conversion, and
Sediment transport together. It therefore cannot attribute the cure to Water
alone. That is a limit, not a defect to repair after seeing the table.

The next candidate question is narrower and more discriminating:

> When complete-state stability is common ground, can transport competence
> select a different final spatial form?

A future campaign would need a fresh extended parcel, a shared stable divisor,
and a strict competence-only pair. None of the lengths, values, forcing
boundaries, or final surfaces in this probe may be reused as campaign
specimens.
